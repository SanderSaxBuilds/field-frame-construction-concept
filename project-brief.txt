FIELD & FRAME - WEBSITE CONCEPT

Independent portfolio sample by Alexandr Khrustalev.
Field & Frame is fictional. This is not a past client project, and no construction services are offered.

Demonstrated scope
Responsive service presentation, clear navigation, an enquiry journey and an accessible HTML structure.

For a real client project
Confirm services, service area, approved photos, business details, page count, enquiry destination, content editing requirements and hosting before fixing scope.
Replace concept copy and reference imagery with client-approved materials.
Connect and test the client's chosen enquiry form or booking system.
Provide editing instructions for the agreed platform.

Contact and payment information are not collected by this sample.
